About Nightjar Coffee Co.

Our Story

Nightjar Coffee Co. started as an idea for a coffee shop that felt personal instead of corporate. The goal was to create a space where students, workers, and neighbors could get a reliable drink and spend time in a calm environment.

Our Mission

We focus on thoughtful service, strong coffee, and a welcoming atmosphere. Instead of offering an overwhelming menu, we keep things focused and make sure the basics are done well.

What Makes Us Different

We try to balance quality with comfort. Some coffee shops feel too formal, while others feel rushed. Nightjar is built around a more steady and approachable experience.

Key Terms

Espresso
A concentrated coffee brewed by forcing hot water through finely ground beans.
Cold Brew
A coffee drink made by steeping grounds in cold water for many hours.
Small-Batch Roasting
A roasting method that focuses on smaller amounts of beans for better control and flavor.
